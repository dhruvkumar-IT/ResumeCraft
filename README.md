# ResumeCraft AI 🚀
### Build a Resume That Gets Noticed

A modern, responsive, and easy-to-use Resume Builder that helps users create professional resumes without registration or complicated steps.

🌐 **Live Website:** [Open ResumeCraft AI](https://dhruvkumar-it.github.io/resumecraft-ai/)

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎨 **15 Professional Templates** – Choose from 15 different resume designs.
- ⚡ **Live Resume Preview** – See your resume update instantly while editing.
- 🖼️ **Profile Photo Upload** – Add your own photo with circle or square shapes.
- 🎯 **Custom Theme Colours** – Personalize your resume with your preferred colours.
- 🎓 **Education Section** – Add multiple educational qualifications.
- 💼 **Work Experience** – Include your professional experience and responsibilities.
- 🛠️ **Skills Section** – Showcase your skills and proficiency levels.
- 💻 **Projects Section** – Highlight your personal and professional projects.
- 🏆 **Certifications & Achievements** – Display your accomplishments.
- 💾 **Save & Load Resume** – Save and retrieve your resume using browser storage.
- 📄 **PDF Export** – Print or save your resume as a PDF.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Designed for different screen sizes.
- 🔓 **No Registration Required** – Start creating your resume immediately.
- 🆓 **Free to Use** – No subscription or premium plans.

## 🚀 Getting Started

You can use ResumeCraft AI directly through the live website.

Alternatively, run it locally:

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Data Storage

- No account registration is required.
- Resume information is stored locally in the user's browser.
- Resume data is not uploaded to a central resume storage server by this version.
- Clearing browser storage may remove saved resumes.

**Note:** Avoid using shared devices for storing personal resume information.
